Section 01

Event Overview & Morning vs. Evening Dynamic

Design & Sports Fest 2026 is a comprehensive, one-day departmental festival bringing together the entire Department of Graphic Design across both shifts: BFA Morning and BFA Evening (8 academic classes total: 1st, 3rd, 5th, and 7th Semesters of Morning and Evening). The festival features an electrifying 8-over Cricket Derby, an 8-team Girls Badminton Knockout Tournament, a Senior Campaign Design Competition (4 teams from 5th & 7th Semesters) creating one unified fest brand, a separate dedicated Jersey Design contest, student-run creative entrepreneurship stalls, and collaborative multimedia production.

Cricket Derby (8 Overs)

A flagship clash between BFA Morning (12 players) and BFA Evening (12 players). One high-intensity match of 8 overs per side decides the departmental champion.

Girls Badminton Knockout (8 Pairs)

4 pairs from BFA Morning and 4 pairs from BFA Evening compete in a streamlined, fast-paced single-elimination knockout tournament (Quarterfinals → Semifinals → Grand Final).

Campaign Design (5th & 7th Sem)

Senior creative showcase featuring 4 teams (2 from Morning, 2 from Evening) representing the 5th and 7th Semesters, competing to craft the one official unified fest campaign identity, logos, social media campaign, and stage signage.

Dedicated Jersey Design Contest

A separate design category to craft the official athletic jerseys for the derby: custom kits for the BFA Morning Team and BFA Evening Team.

Unified Media Production

Balanced media crew with 2 Photographers from Morning and 2 Videographers from Evening documenting the action, interviews, and official recap aftermovie.

Creative & Food Stalls

Student-led enterprises featuring curated food, beverages, handmade bouquets, crochet work, and creative merchandise with custom stall presentations.

Girls Badminton: Quick Knockout Schedule

To keep the tournament intense, exciting, and time-efficient, an 8-team single-elimination bracket is scheduled across 7 matches (~90 minutes total play time).

Tournament Format: Quarterfinals are played to 15 rally points (sudden death at 14-all). Semifinals and Grand Final are played to 21 regulation rally points. This ensures swift match turnaround and high spectator energy.

Venue Allocation & Safety Guidelines

Carefully divided between outdoor open lawns and the indoor gymnasium (subject to formal university approval).

Designated Venue Activities Hosted Facilities & Setup Requirements Notes & Access Controls
Main University Ground Cricket Derby (Morning vs. Evening · 8 Overs) · Food & Art Stalls · Stage & Award Ceremony · General Audience Marked boundary rope, commentary desk with dual microphones (1 Male, 1 Female commentator), stall canopies, spectator seating Central open-air hub. Wide open layout for free student movement.
University Gymnasium Badminton Knockout Tournament (8 Doubles Pairs) Regulation indoor court markings, tournament net, referee high chair, player warm-up bench Protected indoor environment ensuring comfort and privacy for female competitors.

Mandatory Campus Property Protection Protocols

  • Zero Property Damage: Walls, sports surfaces, glass panes, and furniture must not have tape or permanent fasteners attached.
  • Careful Installations: All banners, signage, and stall structures must be freestanding or secured with non-damaging ties.
  • Litter-Free Mandate: Dedicated color-coded recycling and waste bins installed every 15 meters around stall zones.
  • Full Restoration: No committee departs until the ground and gymnasium are returned in pristine, spotless condition.

Section 11

Event-Day Management & Match Day Schedule

Master Operational Principles

  • Unified Command: Joint Morning & Evening Main Leads maintain supreme coordination oversight.
  • Strict Schedule Adherence: Match start times, stall opening hours, and presentation windows run according to fixed whistles.
  • Faculty Supervision: Faculty advisers will be on-site throughout the day for guidance and protocol support.
  • Rapid Resolution Desk: A central helpdesk handles player inquiries, visitor credentials, and equipment replacements immediately.
  • Zero Trash Left Behind: Complete campus cleanup and inventory return must be confirmed before any team is formally dismissed.
